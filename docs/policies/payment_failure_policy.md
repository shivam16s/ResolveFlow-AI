# Payment Failure Policy

Policy ID: payment_failure_policy
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: ConnectCare Telecom Billing Operations

## Purpose

This policy defines how agents handle failed, pending, or uncertain payments.

## Payment States

- Paid: payment is successful and linked to an invoice.
- Pending: payment is initiated but not confirmed by the gateway.
- Failed: payment was rejected or reversed.
- Disputed: the payment or invoice is under review.

## Agent Procedure

The agent must check invoice history and payment records before giving a billing answer. The agent must not claim a payment succeeded unless a successful payment record exists.

## Customer Guidance

For pending payments, ask the customer to wait up to 24 hours before retrying unless the gateway marks the transaction as failed. For failed payments, the customer may retry with another payment method.

## Required Evidence

The agent must cite invoice ID, payment ID, amount, payment status, payment date, and method.

## Escalation

Escalate if the customer was debited but ConnectCare has no payment record, payment status is inconsistent, or the issue involves more than one failed retry.
