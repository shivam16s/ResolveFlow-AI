# Refund Policy

Policy ID: refund_policy
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: ConnectCare Telecom Billing Operations

## Purpose

This policy defines when customer payments may be refunded instead of credited to a future bill.

## Eligibility

A refund may be issued when one of these conditions is true:

- A duplicate payment is confirmed for the same customer, amount, and billing date.
- A payment was collected after account cancellation was completed.
- A billing adjustment was approved by a human supervisor.

## Refund Window

Refund requests must be raised within 30 days of the payment date. Older requests require human review.

## Automatic Limit

The agent may not automatically issue cash refunds. For MVP behavior, the agent may create a refund-review ticket and may apply an account credit when another policy allows it.

## Required Evidence

The agent must collect payment ID, invoice ID, payment date, amount, customer ID, and refund reason.

## Escalation

Escalate when the customer requests a cash refund, the amount is greater than INR 500, the payment is older than 30 days, or ownership of the payment cannot be verified.
