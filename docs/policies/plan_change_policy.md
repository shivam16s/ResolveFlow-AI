# Plan Change Policy

Policy ID: plan_change_policy
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: ConnectCare Telecom Plan Operations

## Purpose

This policy defines rules for upgrades, downgrades, and plan changes.

## Eligibility

A customer may change plans when:

- The account is active.
- There is no unpaid overdue invoice.
- The requested plan is available in the customer's service location.
- The customer confirms the new monthly price and speed.

## Upgrade Rules

Plan upgrades may be scheduled immediately. Proration starts from the activation date.

## Downgrade Rules

Plan downgrades take effect from the next billing cycle. If the account is under a promotional lock-in, the agent must disclose any cancellation or downgrade fee.

## Required Evidence

The agent must cite current plan, requested plan, monthly price, speed, and effective date.

## Escalation

Escalate when the customer disputes a fee, requests backdated plan change, has pending cancellation, or has an unresolved service complaint tied to the plan.
