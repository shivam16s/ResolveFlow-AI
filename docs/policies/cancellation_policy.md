# Cancellation Policy

Policy ID: cancellation_policy
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: ConnectCare Telecom Retention Operations

## Purpose

This policy defines how the agent responds when a customer says they want to cancel service.

## Required Checks

Before cancellation guidance, the agent must check:

- Customer identity and account status.
- Current plan and cancellation fee.
- Open service issues or billing disputes.
- Recent outage or unresolved ticket history.
- Churn risk level.

## Retention Handling

If the customer has an unresolved billing or service issue, the agent should acknowledge the cancellation intent and attempt to resolve the active issue first. The agent may offer technician scheduling or eligible credits when supported by policy.

## Allowed Actions

The agent may explain cancellation steps, create a retention ticket, or prepare a handoff. The agent must not complete cancellation automatically in the MVP.

## Required Evidence

The audit trail must include the cancellation trigger message, active issues, account status, and any policy-backed retention action.

## Escalation

Escalate when the customer explicitly asks for immediate cancellation, is high value or critical risk, has an unresolved complaint, or asks for a fee waiver.
